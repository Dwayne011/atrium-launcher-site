# Atrium Launcher: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 12 November 2026.*

Atrium Launcher ("Atrium", "the app") is an Android home screen made by Dwayne Edwards.
Contact: dwayne.edwards89@googlemail.com

## The short version

- Atrium has no accounts of its own, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
- What Atrium reads on your phone stays on your phone. The developer does not receive it.
- Atrium contacts outside services only to fetch what you have switched on (weather, news, prices,
  scores, travel times, your own mail), and sends them only what the request needs.
- Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

## What Atrium reads on your phone, and why

Every item below is optional and off until you grant it. All of it is used on the device only.

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Installed apps | To show your apps, their names and icons, and to search them. |
| Notifications (notification access) | Notification dots and counts, and cards for navigation, timers, music, messages, deliveries and Live Updates. Notification content is not stored beyond what a card is showing and is never sent anywhere. |
| Location, including in the background | Local weather, travel time for your commute, and showing a ticket or loyalty card when you arrive at its place. |
| Calendar | The next-event card, the agenda widget, calendar search, and adding a fixture you choose to your calendar. |
| Contacts | Finding people when you search. |
| Photos, videos and audio, and folders you pick | Finding files when you search. |
| Health Connect (steps, sleep, heart rate and the other measures you allow) | The health chips and health dashboards. Health data is never sent off the device and is never used for anything else. |
| App usage (usage access) | The screen-time chip and dashboard. |
| Bluetooth | Battery level of connected headphones and watches. |
| Accessibility service | Only to lock the screen when you double-tap. It reads nothing on screen and observes none of your actions. |
| Biometrics / screen lock | To confirm it is you before showing a pass or opening Private Space. |

## Your Google account (Gmail and Google Drive)

If you sign in with Google, Atrium asks for:

- **Gmail (read, and modify):** to count unread mail, show your unread list, find delivery and ticket
  emails for the parcel and ticket cards, and mark an email as read when you swipe it.
- **Google Drive (file names and details only):** so your Drive files appear in search. Atrium does
  not read the contents of Drive files.

This data goes directly between your phone and Google. It is processed on your phone, is not sent
to the developer or to anyone else, and is not used for advertising. You can sign out in Atrium's
settings, and remove access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

Atrium's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Other mail accounts and keys you enter

- **Mail accounts added with an app password (IMAP):** the address and password are stored on your
  phone and used only to sign in to that mail provider.
- **eToro keys:** stored on your phone and sent only to eToro. They are
  never written to a backup file.

## Services Atrium contacts

Atrium sends each service only what the request needs. Each service sees your IP address, as any
website does, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

| Feature | Service | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weather, rain, air quality | Open-Meteo, RainViewer | Your approximate or precise location |
| Commute, and travel time to a calendar event | OSRM, Photon (Komoot), Transport for London | Your location and the destination: your saved places, or the place written on the event |
| Parking near an event | OpenStreetMap's Overpass servers; the MotoBays bay list (a file on GitHub) | The event's map position. The bay list is downloaded whole, so nothing about you is sent for it. |
| Prices and markets | Yahoo Finance, Nasdaq | The symbols you follow |
| eToro portfolio | eToro | Your eToro keys |
| Sports | ESPN, OpenF1, Jolpica, Formula 1 live timing | The teams and competitions you follow |
| News feed | Atrium's news service (hosted on Cloudflare), publishers' own feeds and Google News | The topics you chose. No account or device identifier. |
| Web search suggestions | Google, Bing | What you type in the search box, when web suggestions are on |
| Mail | Google, or your mail provider | Your sign-in, as described above |

Pictures in emails and articles are loaded from the sender's or publisher's own servers.

## What is stored, and where

Your layout, settings, loyalty cards, tickets, and cached weather, prices and parcels are stored on
your phone. A backup file is created only when you ask for one and is saved where you choose.
Uninstalling Atrium removes everything it stored.

Atrium's news service keeps no record of who asked for which topics.

## Crash reports

If Atrium crashes it shows you a report on the phone. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share it.

## Children

Atrium is not directed at children under 13.
